Smart Travel Starts Here

Essential travel tips for a smooth and memorable journey across India.

Packing Essentials

Carry light cotton clothes for summer, warm jackets for winter, and comfortable footwear.

Budget Travel Tips

Use Indian Railways, book hotels early, and enjoy affordable street food.

Safety Tips

Use official transport, avoid scams, and keep emergency contacts handy.

Cultural Etiquette

Dress modestly in temples, remove footwear before entering homes, and greet with “Namaste”.
